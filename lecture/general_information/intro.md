# Intro 

Learn more about the objectives, the competencies, materials and the schedule of this course:

## Objectives 📍
Religion has always been—and continues to be—profoundly intertwined with media. Processes of digital transformation, in particular, extend, reshape, and reconfigure existing media forms. It is therefore incumbent upon theology to reflect continuously on media in their ongoing transformation and to further develop their theological relevance. Through the close reading of key texts, this seminar offers an introduction to major trajectories of theological media reflection. Together, we will explore, discuss, and critically examine various dimensions of a theologically informed theory of media.

## Competencies 🚀

Students will be able to...
- reconstruct the content and argumentative structure of media-theoretical texts;
- explain central concepts of a theologically oriented media theory;
- independently introduce and contextualize a media-theoretical text;
- critically engage with theological approaches to media theory;
- articulate and document their own reflections on the texts in the form of a portfolio.
